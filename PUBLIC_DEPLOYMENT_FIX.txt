MAKE THE FOOD APP LINK PUBLIC
=============================
The supplied link is a protected Vercel deployment URL.

1. In Vercel, open the food-app project.
2. Open Deployments.
3. Open the newest successful deployment made from the main branch.
4. Choose Promote to Production if it is not already Production.
5. Open Settings > Deployment Protection.
6. Do not protect the Production environment with Vercel Authentication. Preview/deployment URLs may stay protected.
7. Copy the Production domain shown on the project Overview page. Use that link, not the long unique preview URL.

UPLOAD RULE
Extract the ZIP and upload its CONTENTS to the repository root. Do not upload the enclosing folder.
